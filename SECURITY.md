# Security and Privacy Policy

STEMEd+ Commons publishes information about people, programs, and opportunities, so we treat
**privacy exposures** (for example, personal information about someone appearing in a listing, a
published data file, or a repository's history without their choosing to share it) as security
issues, in addition to ordinary software vulnerabilities.

## Reporting a problem

**Please do not open a public issue.** Instead, either:

* use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting: go to the affected repository's **Security** tab
  and choose **Report a vulnerability**; or
* use the contact form at **https://stemedplus.org/contact-us/**.

Please include what you found, where (repository, file, commit, page, or URL), and how to
reproduce it. For privacy exposures, describe the location but **do not copy the sensitive
information into your report.**

## What to expect

* Acknowledgement within **3 business days**.
* For privacy exposures, we will remove the content from the live site and repository as quickly
  as possible and, where needed, rewrite repository history. Copies made by others before removal
  cannot be recalled.
* We will credit reporters who wish to be credited once a fix is released.

## Supported versions

Only each repository's current `main` branch and its published site are supported.
